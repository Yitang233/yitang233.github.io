agent-rewind: Selective Recovery for Multi-Agent LLM Workflows

UC San Diego · Team project for CSE 291A (Systems for LLMs and AI Agents)

  • When a multi-agent workflow fails, it reruns only the steps whose inputs changed. It tracks this through Merkle-hashed records of what each step read.
  • Compared with a full restart: about 4x fewer reruns, up to 2x faster recovery, and about 9x fewer repeated irreversible actions.
